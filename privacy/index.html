Privacy policy

What Word Laddered and this website do with your data: almost nothing.

Last updated 16 October 2026.

The short version

Word Laddered and this website don't collect your personal data. There are no ads, no tracking, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. Your progress is stored on your device.

What the game stores

Your progress, settings, avatar, cosmetics and scores are stored on your device. None of it is sent to Quinde Studio.

What Apple handles

Some features use Apple services. Apple's own privacy terms apply to them, and we can't see the data they handle.

  • Game Center provides leaderboards and achievements, and uses your Game Center name and scores.
  • iCloud syncs your progress between your devices through your iCloud account.
  • Notifications are reminders scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in Settings at any time.

Bug reports and email

If you report a bug from the game, it opens an email addressed to us that you can read and edit before you send it. If you email us, we use your address only to reply, and we delete the conversation if you ask.

This website

This site uses no cookies, no analytics, and no third-party scripts, fonts or embeds. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, for security and reliability. We don't use them to identify or track visitors.

Children

Because we don't collect personal information, we don't knowingly collect it from children.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you from the game, so there is nothing to access or delete. For emails you've sent us, write to privacy@quindestudio.com and we'll help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and say what changed.

Contact

Quinde Studio, privacy@quindestudio.com.
