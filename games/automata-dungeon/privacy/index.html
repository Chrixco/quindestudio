Automata Dungeon

Automata Dungeon privacy policy

What Automata Dungeon does with your data: it stays on your device.

Last updated 25 November 2026.

The short version

Automata Dungeon doesn't collect, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. Your progress and your levels stay on your device, and the only online service the game uses is Apple's Game Center.

What the game stores

Your progress (unlocked levels, best step counts and coins), the levels and patterns you make, and your settings, such as sound, music and simulation speed, are stored on your device. Levels you make are also saved as files in the game's folder, which you can see in the Files app.

None of it is sent to Quinde Studio. Settings > Reset Progress clears your progress, and deleting the game removes everything it stored.

What Apple handles

When you open the game it signs you in to Game Center, if you use it. The game keeps your Game Center player ID and name on your device. Game Center is provided by Apple, Apple's own privacy terms apply to it, and we can't see the data it handles.

iCloud sync isn't switched on in the current version, so your progress stays on the device you play on.

Sharing levels

When you choose to share or export a level, the game creates a small file describing it and opens the iOS share sheet. You decide where it goes. The community library doesn't upload anything yet; if that changes, we'll update this policy first.

Email

If you email us, we use your address only to reply, and we delete the conversation if you ask.

Children

Because the game doesn't collect personal information, it doesn't collect it from children.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you from the game, so there is nothing to access or delete. For emails you've sent us, write to privacy@quindestudio.com and we'll help.

Changes

Automata Dungeon is in development. If a later version adds online features, such as iCloud sync or the community library, we'll update this policy and the date above before it ships, and say what changed.

Contact

Quinde Studio, privacy@quindestudio.com. For how this website handles data, see the studio privacy policy.
