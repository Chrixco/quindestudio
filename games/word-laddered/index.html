Three rules.

A turn takes a second. Planning the next one takes longer.

  • Slide

    Swipe in any direction and every tile slides as far as it can. After each move, a new small letter appears.

    AC
    AC

  • Merge

    Tiles that touch and sit next to each other in the alphabet merge into the letter above the higher one. A and B make C. B and C make D. That is how you climb.

    A+B=C
    B+C=D

  • Vanish

    Fill a whole row or column with one letter and the line vanishes. Matching letters don't merge, so clearing a line takes planning.

On your iPhone

  • Slide, merge, climb

    Swipe to slide the board. Neighbouring letters merge into the next one up, so A and B make C. Every merge takes you one step higher, and a new small letter appears after each move.

  • A path to follow

    Leo's Path is a trail of levels through a painted forest, with Leo as your guide. Beside it grows a skill tree with more than a hundred skills to unlock as you play.

  • Make it yours

    Make an avatar and unlock new looks as you play. Earn XP towards your next level.

  • Pick your pace

    Classic is a pure puzzle with no power-ups or undo. WordSpace lets you draft power-ups from the skill tree. Flappy Letter and Orbit put the same letter rules on a flying course and a round board. Choose a 5 by 5 grid, or a 6 by 6 grid for more room to plan.

  • Something new every day

    Tap to claim a daily reward, then take on three daily challenges. Finish all three for bonus rewards.

Questions

When can I play it?

Word Laddered is coming soon to iPhone and iPad. Write to us for launch news.

How much will it cost?

The price hasn't been announced.

Are there ads?

No. Word Laddered has no ads, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. The privacy policy explains what it stores and where.

How high can I climb?

All the way to Z. A merge that would go past Z isn't possible.

What clears a line?

Fill a whole row or column with the same letter and the line vanishes. Matching letters don't merge, so clearing a line takes planning.
