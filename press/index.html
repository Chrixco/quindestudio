About the game

In one line

Word Laddered is a calm swipe puzzle for iPhone and iPad: slide the board, merge neighbouring letters, and climb the alphabet.

In a paragraph

In Word Laddered, every tile is a letter. Swipe to slide the board, and tiles that touch and sit next to each other in the alphabet merge into the letter above the higher one: A and B make C, B and C make D. Fill a row or column with a single letter and it vanishes. Players climb Leo's Path, a set of skills they unlock as they play, with Classic, WordSpace, Flappy Letter and Orbit modes, a daily challenge, power-ups, avatars, Home Screen widgets, and Game Center leaderboards.

About the studio

Quinde Studio is an independent studio making games and apps, including the puzzle Word Laddered for iPhone and iPad. Word Laddered has no ads, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.
