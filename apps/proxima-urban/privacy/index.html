Proxima Urban privacy policy

What the Proxima Urban app does with your data: it stays with you and your team.

Last updated 25 November 2026.

In short

Proxima Urban does not collect your data. The app has no sign-in of its own, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your projects, quotes, invoices and messages stay on your devices, in the folder you choose and, if you use it, in your own iCloud account.

Where your data is kept

Workspace folder. Projects, documents and settings are files in the folder you choose: on this device or in iCloud Drive. Everyone you give access to that folder can read them.

CloudKit. When an owner moves a workspace to CloudKit, its data is stored in the owner's iCloud account and shared only with the people the owner invites. Chat messages are stored encrypted. Apple's iCloud terms and privacy policy apply.

On this device. Your name, initials and preferences are kept in the app's settings on each device.

Services you connect

Nothing is sent to these services unless you connect them, and you can disconnect each one:

  • Apple Calendar: with your permission, the app adds its own events (meetings, deadlines) to a calendar on your device.
  • Google Calendar: after you sign in with Google, the app writes the same events to your Google calendar. The sign-in token is kept in the keychain; Disconnect removes it and revokes access at Google.
  • InvoiceXpress: when you send an invoice, its contents (client name, address, VAT number, amounts) go to your InvoiceXpress account. The API key is kept in the keychain.
  • TED (EU tenders): tender searches send your search terms to the EU's public TED service; no personal data is sent.

These services' own privacy policies apply to the data you send them.

Diagnostics

Crash and performance reports from the system (MetricKit) are kept on this device. They are sent to no one unless you share them yourself (You → About → Share diagnostic reports).

Team chat

The chat is visible only to members of your workspace. You can report a message (it opens an email to your workspace's admins and to us) and mute a person on your device. Owners and admins can remove members from the workspace.

Deleting your data

Delete projects in the app, or delete the workspace folder. To delete CloudKit data, the owner moves the workspace back to a folder and removes the app's data in Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage storage. Disconnect Google Calendar and InvoiceXpress in Company → Integrations or You. Removing the app deletes its settings on that device.

Children

Proxima Urban is a business app for professional firms and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy when the app changes how it handles data, and show the date of the latest version here. Proxima Urban is made by Christian Corral (Quinde Studio). Privacy questions: privacy@quindestudio.com; help with the app: support@quindestudio.com.
