Dusk support

Help with Dusk, from the people who made it.

Contact us

Email support@quindestudio.com. Tell us your Mac model and macOS version, and what happened. A screenshot helps. We read every message.

Common questions

Where is Dusk after I open it?

Dusk lives in the menu bar: look for the moon at the top right of your screen. It has no Dock icon. Click the moon for controls, or open Settings from there.

How do I start Dusk when I log in?

Open Dusk's Settings > General and turn on Open at login. If macOS asks, allow Dusk in System Settings > General > Login Items.

The colours look too warm, or change twice.

Turn off Night Shift (System Settings > Displays > Night Shift) and True Tone, so they don't stack with Dusk.

Sunset and sunrise times are wrong.

Check that Location Services is on for Dusk in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services, or type your coordinates into Dusk's Schedule settings.

How do I switch it off quickly?

Press Control, Option, Command and D from any app, or pause it from the menu bar for an hour, three hours or until morning.

How do I get my normal colours back?

Switch Dusk off from the menu bar, or quit it. Your display returns to normal straight away.

What data does Dusk collect?

None. Read the Dusk privacy policy for the details.
