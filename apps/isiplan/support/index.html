Isiplan support

Help with Isiplan, from the people who made it.

Contact us

Email support@quindestudio.com. Tell us your device and system version, whether your workspace is a folder or in CloudKit, and what happened. A screenshot helps. In the app, You > About > Contact support opens the same email.

Common questions

How do I start?

On first launch, enter your name and initials, choose a folder for the workspace (in iCloud Drive to use it on all your devices, or on this device), pick the template closest to your practice and press Start. Company details can be filled in later in Company > Profile.

How do I work with colleagues?

Share the workspace folder in iCloud Drive, or move the workspace to CloudKit in Company > Workspace and invite each colleague from Company > People with the email of their Apple Account.

Someone in the chat is being abusive.

Right-click or long-press the message and choose Report message, which emails your workspace's admins and us, or Mute to hide that person's messages on your device. Owners and admins can remove members in Company > People.

My meetings don't appear in my calendar.

Open You > Calendars and allow calendar access, or connect Google Calendar there. On iPhone and iPad you can also allow access in Settings > Privacy & Security > Calendars.

How do I see the tips again?

Open You > About and choose Show the tips again. They return the next time the app starts. Every page also has a help button (?) in its toolbar.

What data does Isiplan collect?

None. Your data stays in your workspace folder and your own iCloud account. Read the privacy policy.
