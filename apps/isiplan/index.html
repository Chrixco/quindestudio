About

Isiplan is the workspace of a small planning or design practice: the projects you are chasing and delivering, the tasks and meetings around them, quotes and invoices, and a chat for the team.

It's a native app for iPhone, iPad and Mac, built with Swift and SwiftUI. Your data stays in a folder you choose and in your own iCloud account, not on our servers.

What it looks like

Screens show a fictional practice and fictional projects.

  • Every project on one board

    Follow each project from the first enquiry to delivery. Cards show the client, value, deadline, owner and unread messages, and the totals above tell you what is waiting for a decision and what is in delivery.

  • Your week, and the whole year

    My work gathers your tasks, meetings and messages for today and this week. The Timeline lays every project out as a Gantt chart, with deadlines that need attention flagged at the top.

  • In your pocket on site

    Check tasks, open a project and see the next meetings from a site visit or a client's office. Everything stays in step with the Mac and iPad.

  • Plan ahead with the Timeline

    Zoom from weeks to years, colour projects by stage and click a bar to open the project. A calendar, an agenda and a workload view sit one tab away.

Built for teams

  • One shared workspace

    Share a folder in iCloud Drive, or move the workspace to CloudKit and invite colleagues with their Apple Account. Roles decide who manages the team, prices and invoices.

  • Chat where the work is

    A conversation for every project, a team channel and direct messages, with mentions, reactions and messages you can turn into tasks.

  • Meetings and minutes

    Plan meetings, take the protocol during them and send it as a document. Meetings and deadlines can appear in Apple Calendar or Google Calendar.

  • Quotes and invoices

    Build quotes from your own services and rates, and issue invoices and documents as Word files, in your practice's colours and logo.

  • Public tenders

    Watch the EU's TED notices for the countries and services you work in, and turn a promising tender into a lead.

  • Made for each screen

    A board and a timeline on the Mac and iPad, and the essentials on iPhone, with help on every page.

Questions

Who is Isiplan for?

Small urban-planning, architecture and consultancy practices that run projects for public and private clients. Templates set up the stages, project folders and client types of each kind of practice.

Where is my data stored?

In the folder you choose, on your device or in iCloud Drive, and in your own iCloud account if you move the workspace to CloudKit. Quinde Studio doesn't receive it. See the privacy policy.

Do I need an account?

No. Teams use their Apple Accounts to share a workspace through iCloud.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS and iPadOS 26.1 or later, and Macs with macOS 26.1 or later.

When can I get it?

Isiplan is in development. Write to hello@quindestudio.com to hear when it's available.

Where can I get help?

See Isiplan support and the privacy policy.
