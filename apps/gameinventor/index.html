About

GameInventor is a desktop app for designing complete tabletop games: cards and decks, boards (freeform, square or hex), standees and meeples, dice, tokens, counters, player boards and rulebooks.

Edit your card data and see it render live, test the game on a virtual table, then export print-ready files with bleed and crop marks, or a print-and-play sheet you can cut at home.

From idea to print

  1. Design

    Lay out cards, paint boards, shape tokens and standees, and write the rulebook.

  2. Test

    Play your game on a virtual table as you design it, and keep notes on what you find.

  3. Print

    Export print-ready PDFs with bleed and crop marks, or a print-and-play sheet.

What it looks like

  • Paint the board

    Paint hex or square boards tile by tile, place blueprints such as lakes, forests and mountain ranges, and keep track of how many cells are left to paint.

  • Then see it in 3D

    A perspective preview shows the board at the height of its terrain. Switch between perspective, top and side views to check how it will look on the table.

  • Design the card

    Set the print size, corner radius, bleed and safe margin once, and see every card in a data table below the preview. Warnings tell you when artwork is too low in resolution or text sits too close to the edge.

  • Write the rulebook

    Pages and facing pages with an outline, and live references to your components, so the rules and the pieces stay in step.

  • Show it off

    Render a hero shot of your game: on the board, on the table, in a box, as a card fan or flat lay. Choose the surface, the light and an image size for a social post or a final render.

  • Play the table

    Set up a table for two or more players, move pieces and keep a log of what you notice, such as a rule that is unclear or a piece that is too strong. Seeds keep sessions repeatable.

  • Shape your pieces

    Tokens, standees and counters start from a shape and a size, then get a face and a name. Nest them on punchboards and fold paper dice.

Under the hood

  • Start from a game type

    Create a project from one of ten game types, each set up with the components that kind of game needs.

  • Autosave and recovery

    Your project saves as you go and recovers after a crash.

  • Print pre-flight

    Checks warn you before you print: resolution, margins, bleed.

  • Exact dice odds

    Analyse a dice pool exactly instead of guessing.

Questions

What can I design?

Cards and decks, boards (freeform, square or hex), standees and meeples, dice, tokens, counters, player boards and rulebooks.

What do I get out of it?

Print-ready PDFs with bleed, crop marks and embedded fonts, plus a print-and-play sheet, and Showcase renders for sharing.

Is it finished?

No. GameInventor is in development. The screenshots show the current app, and more is on the way.

Which platforms?

It is a desktop app. Mac, Windows and Linux builds are planned.

Can I try it?
