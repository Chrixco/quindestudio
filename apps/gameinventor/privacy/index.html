GameInventor privacy policy

What GameInventor does with your data: your projects stay on your computer.

Last updated 25 November 2026.

The short version

GameInventor doesn't collect, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics, no telemetry and no third-party SDKs that collect data. Your projects stay on your computer. The only time GameInventor connects to the internet is when you ask it to import a Google Sheet.

What GameInventor stores

Each project is a folder you choose, by default in Documents > GameInventor. It holds the project itself, the images and fonts you import, your exports, automatic backups and a recovery copy in case the app quits unexpectedly.

Your preferences, panel layout, recent projects list and last export settings are kept by the app on your computer. Your personal terrain library is saved in GameInventor's settings folder: Application Support on a Mac, AppData on Windows, and .config on Linux.

If you type a designer name in Settings, GameInventor adds it as the author of the PDF and Word files you export. Leave it empty if you'd rather it didn't.

Importing from Google

If you paste a link to a Google Sheet, GameInventor downloads that sheet directly from Google. It works only with sheets shared as "anyone with the link", never signs in to your Google account and sends no cookies or passwords. Google sees the request, including your IP address, and Google's own privacy policy applies to it. Nothing passes through Quinde Studio.

To import a Google Doc, GameInventor opens the document's download link in your web browser, and you import the downloaded file yourself.

Bug reports

Help > Report a bug builds a report on your computer. By default it includes your app version, operating system, window size and the last few errors, with your home folder removed from file paths; you can switch both off. It never includes your project. GameInventor doesn't send the report anywhere: you copy it or save it as a file and decide where it goes.

Email

If you email us, we use your address only to reply, and we delete the conversation if you ask.

Children

Because GameInventor doesn't collect personal information, it doesn't collect it from children.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you from GameInventor, so there is nothing to access or delete. For emails you've sent us, write to privacy@quindestudio.com and we'll help.

Changes

GameInventor is in development. If a later version adds anything that uses the network, such as store features or an option to send bug reports, we'll update this policy and the date above before it ships, and say what changed.

Contact

Quinde Studio, privacy@quindestudio.com. For how this website handles data, see the studio privacy policy.
