About

RepoSync lives in the menu bar and, every two hours by default, commits and pushes the git repositories in the folders you choose. Small change sets go out silently. Bigger or unusual ones wait for your approval.

The same panel shows which repos are open in your terminals and, if you like, your Claude Code usage limits.

    How it works

    1. It checks every two hours

      RepoSync looks at each repo in the folders you watch. You can change the interval. The minimum is 15 minutes.

    2. Small changes go out silently

      It commits with your prefix and the time, then pushes. A notification tells you, if you want one.

    3. Big or risky changes wait

      Each approval card says what would go out, who could see it, and whether it can be taken back. Push it, skip it, or decide later.

    What it looks like

    • Risky changes wait for your OK

      Cards explain what is about to happen in plain words: how many files and lines, who can see the repo (private, or open to the whole internet), and whether a later commit can undo it. Secret-like files such as .env are flagged before they leave your Mac.

    • Push what you're working on

      RepoSync sees which repos are open in your terminals. Tick the ones you want and push them in one go.

    • See your Claude limits before you hit them

      Session and weekly limits, your pace and token counts, read from Claude Code on your Mac. Turn the Claude usage panel on or off in Settings.

    • Never signs in to your Claude account

      RepoSync reads what Claude Code already saves on this Mac. Your password and tokens stay where they are.

    Details that matter

    • Pick your repositories

      Sync every repo in your watched folders, or only the ones you tick. Scan up to four levels deep.

    • Always asks about risky files

      Deleted files and secret-like files, such as .env files and keys, always need approval.

    • Readable commits

      Commit messages carry your prefix, the time, and what changed, so the history makes sense later.

    • Opens at login

      Start it once and forget it.

    Questions

    What counts as a big change?

    By default: more than 10 files, more than 300 added or removed lines, more than 5 unpushed commits, or a file over 50 MB. You can change every limit in Settings.

    Will it push my secrets?

    No. Secret-like files, such as .env files and keys, always wait for your approval, and so do deleted files.

    Which repos does it sync?

    Every repo in the folders you watch, or only the ones you tick. It scans up to four levels deep.

    Does it need my Claude account?

    No. If you turn Claude usage on, RepoSync reads what Claude Code already saves on your Mac. It never signs in.

    Can a push be undone?

    Each approval card says. Usually a later commit can revert it, and the history keeps a copy.

    Which Macs does it run on?

    macOS 14 or later, on Apple silicon.
