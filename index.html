Slide letters. Climb the alphabet.

Word Laddered is a calm swipe puzzle for iPhone and iPad. Slide the board, merge neighbouring letters, and see how high you can climb.

Use the arrow keys to slide the tiles. Neighbouring letters merge into the next letter up.

Three rules.

A turn takes a second. Planning the next one takes longer.

  • Slide

    Swipe in any direction and every tile slides as far as it can. After each move, a new small letter appears.

    AC
    AC

  • Merge

    Tiles that touch and sit next to each other in the alphabet merge into the letter above the higher one. A and B make C. B and C make D. That is how you climb.

    A+B=C
    B+C=D

  • Vanish

    Fill a whole row or column with one letter and the line vanishes. Matching letters don't merge, so clearing a line takes planning.

    DDDDD

Inside the game

What's in the first release.

  • Four ways to play

    Classic is a pure puzzle with no power-ups or undo. WordSpace lets you draft power-ups from the skill tree. Flappy Letter and Orbit put the same letter rules on a flying course and a round board.

  • Leo's Path

    A path of levels and a skill tree with more than a hundred skills, with Leo as your guide.

  • Daily challenge

    A new challenge each day and a small reward for returning.

  • Power-ups

    Tools for tight boards in WordSpace, drafted from the skill tree.

  • Your avatar

    Make an avatar and unlock new looks as you play.

  • Home Screen widgets

    Your progress, one glance away.

  • Game Center

    Leaderboards and achievements through Game Center.

  • iCloud

    Your progress follows you across your Apple devices.
