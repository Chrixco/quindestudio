The studio

Quinde Studio is an independent studio making games and apps: puzzles and tabletop games, tools for designers and Mac users, and a workspace for planning practices.

  • One job, done well

    Each app does one thing and stays out of the way. Each game is easy to learn and slow to master.

  • At home on Apple devices

    Most of what we make runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac, and uses Game Center, iCloud and widgets where they help.

  • Screen and table

    Puzzles to play on a phone, games to play around a table, and a tool to design your own.

  • Private by design

    Word Laddered has no ads, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. Its privacy policy explains what it stores and where.
